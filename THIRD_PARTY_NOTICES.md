# Third-party notices

PseudoPath adapts selected EDS-Pseudo v0.4.0 rule patterns in
`src/pseudopath/_rule_patterns.py`, the entity-boundary cleanup policy in
`src/pseudopath/_entity_cleanup.py`. The training-document splitting policy
in `src/pseudopath/_reader.py` follows EDS-Pseudo's `PseudoReader` and
`subset_doc`. The surrounding implementation is PseudoPath code. EDS-Pseudo
pipeline components and factories are not bundled. The original source is
<https://github.com/aphp/eds-pseudo/tree/v0.4.0>.
